### 30 seconds

Nova Ai-Studio delivers whole projects from one sentence. Marketing teams run a six-stage flywheel with 400+ try-ready capabilities and ~30 templates. Enterprise edition adds tenant isolation and cloud files; smart routing saves up to ~70% on tokens.

### 2 minutes

Teams already use ChatGPT for copy, but **deliverables stay scattered**. Nova turns conversation into **workflow**: research MD, landing HTML, monitoring reports in one project space with preview in the thread.

We focus on **marketing creative**, not generic agents: flywheel stages, templates for campaigns and GEO, unified model pool (GPT, Claude, Gemini, Qwen, Seedream, etc.).

For enterprises: tenant isolation, cloud hubs, admin usage. For cost: routing sends hard steps to flagship models and light work to efficient models.
