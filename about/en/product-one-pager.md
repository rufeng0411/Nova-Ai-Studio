# Nova Ai-Studio 2.0 · Product One-Pager (EN)

## What it is

**Nova Ai-Studio 2.0** is a conversation-driven **AI marketing creative production platform**.

Users describe goals in natural language; the platform delivers **reviewable project files**—research briefs, landing pages, decks, video scripts, social packs—not just chat replies.

## Who it's for

| Segment | Use cases |
|---------|-----------|
| Brand marketing teams | Campaigns, GEO monitoring, competitive research |
| Agencies & studios | Pitch decks, comparison landing pages, social content |
| Solo creators | Templates from outline → PPT → video |
| B2B growth | SaaS growth playbooks, lead-gen landing pages |
| Education | K12 and academic writing (dedicated tab) |
| Enterprise IT | Multi-tenant SaaS or private Docker deploy |

## Three core values

1. **Conversation → project delivery** — isolated workspaces, continuous memory, in-chat preview/download  
2. **Marketing flywheel + scale** — 6 stages, 400+ capabilities, ~30 workflow templates  
3. **Enterprise-ready** — tenant isolation, cloud file hubs, admin usage dashboards  

## vs general chat AI

Chat products **answer**; Nova **delivers whole projects** with organized capabilities, templates, flywheel stages, and file acceptance in the thread.

## Key metrics

- 400+ ready capabilities  
- ~30 workflow templates  
- 6-stage marketing flywheel  
- 150 Open Design systems  
- Up to ~70% token savings via smart routing
